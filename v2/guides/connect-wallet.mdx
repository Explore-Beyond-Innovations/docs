ProofBridge works with one wallet per chain:

- **EVM** (Sepolia): MetaMask, Rainbow, or any RainbowKit-supported wallet
- **Stellar** (Testnet): Freighter and other Stellar Wallets Kit wallets

<Frame caption="The wallet hub — connect one or both chains">
  <img src="/images/v2/connect-01-hub.png" alt="Wallet connection hub" />
</Frame>

## Which wallets do you need?

The wallet of the chain **you deposit from** is required. Connecting both
is recommended:

- as a **bridger**, your deposit wallet is on the order chain and your
  destination address is on the other chain
- as a **maker**, your liquidity lives on the ad chain and you receive on
  the order chain

## Sign in

Connecting prompts a one-time login signature (a challenge — no
transaction, no gas). Linking your second wallet ties both to the same
account so the app can show all your trades and sponsor key-registration
gas for either.

<Frame caption="Login signature prompt">
  <img src="/images/v2/connect-02-login.png" alt="Login challenge signature" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Each trading wallet also needs a one-time
[settlement key registration](/v2/concepts/settlement-keys) before it can
complete trades — the app prompts you at the right moment, or do it up
front on the **BLS Keys** page.
</Note>
