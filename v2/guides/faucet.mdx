Everything runs on testnets — you need test funds on both sides.

## In-app faucet

The **Faucet** page dispenses the test tokens used by the demo routes to
your connected wallet on either chain.

<Frame caption="The faucet page">
  <img src="/images/v2/faucet-01-page.png" alt="Faucet page" />
</Frame>

## Gas

- **Sepolia ETH** — any public Sepolia faucet
- **Testnet XLM** — Stellar Friendbot funds new accounts; the faucet page
  links it

<Note>
Key registration gas is sponsored by the relayer on both chains — you only
need gas for your own deposits (and, for Stellar wallets, the one
self-submitted registration transaction).
</Note>
